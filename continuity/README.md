# Business continuity — Velmora Payments (fictional)

A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and a business continuity / disaster recovery plan (PCA/PRA) for the fictional fintech assessed in this repository, following ISO 22301 principles and the DORA ICT continuity requirements. Written in French.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| [bia-analyse-impact.md](bia-analyse-impact.md) | 6 activities, impact over time (1 h → 1 week), MTPD / RTO / RPO / minimum service level, dependencies, gaps |
| [pca-plan-continuite.md](pca-plan-continuite.md) | Continuity strategies per activity, crisis organisation, ransomware recovery procedure, test plan, mapping to ISO 27001, DORA and ISO 22301 |

How it connects: the BIA starts from the EBIOS RM feared event "platform unavailable for more than 4 h" ([ebios-rm](../ebios-rm/README.md)), and the plan closes maturity findings B-01 (untested backups) and B-02 (no continuity plan).

*Indicative example for portfolio purposes.*
